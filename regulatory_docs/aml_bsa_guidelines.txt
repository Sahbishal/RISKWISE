REGULATORY DOCUMENT: Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) & Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance Standard
ISSUING AUTHORITY: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) / Federal Reserve Board
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-AML-2024-V2
CATEGORY: AML_BSA

SECTION 1: TRANSACTION MONITORING & ANOMALY DETECTION
Financial institutions must maintain automated transaction monitoring systems capable of identifying unusual patterns of account activity. Key monitoring triggers include:
1. Amount Anomalies: Transactions exceeding 3x the customer's 90-day rolling historical transaction average or sudden single transfers exceeding $10,000.
2. Velocity & Frequency Spikes: More than 5 high-value transactions executed within a 24-hour window for accounts previously exhibiting low-frequency usage.
3. Rapid Movement of Funds (Pass-Through Activity): Funds deposited via wire transfer or ACH and subsequently transferred out to high-risk third parties or foreign accounts within 48 hours with minimal residual balance retained.

SECTION 2: ENHANCED DUE DILIGENCE (EDD) FOR HIGH-RISK TRANSACTIONS
When an account triggers multiple anomaly signals (e.g., combined amount spike, rapid velocity, and international destination), compliance personnel must immediately open an internal investigation case. Enhanced Due Diligence requires:
- Re-verifying source of funds and beneficial ownership.
- Requesting underlying trade or transfer documentation.
- Conducting adverse media and sanctions screening.

SECTION 3: CONSOLIDATED RISK SCORING CRITERIA
Risk scores are calculated on a scale of 0 to 100 based on composite anomaly metrics:
- 0 to 39 (LOW): Normal operational variance; routine monitoring.
- 40 to 69 (MEDIUM): Moderately elevated risk; mandatory secondary compliance review.
- 70 to 100 (HIGH / CRITICAL): Severe anomaly cluster; immediate freeze recommendation and mandatory SAR review within 30 days.
