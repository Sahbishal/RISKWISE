REGULATORY DOCUMENT: Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & Know Your Customer (KYC) Guidance
ISSUING AUTHORITY: Federal Financial Institutions Examination Council (FFIEC)
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-KYC-2024-01
CATEGORY: KYC

SECTION 1: CUSTOMER RISK PROFILING
Financial institutions are required to establish customer risk profiles at account onboarding and update them continuously based on transactional behavior. Risk factors include:
- Occupation & Expected Activity: Baseline transaction velocity and average amount must be established during initial CDD.
- Geographic Location: Customers maintaining address links or transferring funds to high-risk foreign jurisdictions require heightened monitoring.
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs): Senior foreign political figures or their immediate family members automatically default to HIGH risk classification.

SECTION 2: ONGOING MONITORING & TRIGGER EVENTS
A customer profile must be re-assessed upon any of the following trigger events:
1. Deviations > 200% from expected monthly volume established during onboarding.
2. Material changes in geographic transaction destinations.
3. Unexplained structural changes in counterparty relationships.

SECTION 3: CONSEQUENCES OF KYC DEFICIENCIES
Failure to update Customer Risk Ratings following severe transactional alerts constitutes a regulatory deficiency under 31 U.S.C. 5318(h), subjecting the institution to civil money penalties and mandatory enforcement action.
