REGULATORY DOCUMENT: High-Risk Jurisdictions & Geographic Anomaly Framework
ISSUING AUTHORITY: Financial Action Task Force (FATF) / OFAC Compliance Standards
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-FATF-2024-04
CATEGORY: FATF_SANCTIONS

SECTION 1: GEOGRAPHIC ANOMALY IDENTIFICATION
Transactions originating from or terminating in jurisdictions subject to FATF Call for Action or Increased Monitoring require heightened scrutiny. Key indicators include:
- Uncharacteristic foreign wire transfers to non-cooperative offshore financial centers.
- IP address geolocation mismatch exceeding 2,000 miles from registered customer address within a 6-hour period.
- Intermediary banking routes traversing sanctioned or high-risk transit hubs.

SECTION 2: AUTOMATED RISK WEIGHTING FOR JURISDICTIONAL ANOMALIES
In automated risk scoring models, geographic anomalies trigger specific contribution weights:
- Primary High-Risk Destination (e.g., sanctioned territory or non-FATF compliant jurisdiction): +30 to +40 points.
- Rapid IP / Device Geolocation Jump: +20 to +25 points.
- Structuring across foreign corridors: +25 points.

SECTION 3: MANDATORY SANCTIONS ESCALATION PROTOCOL
If a transaction matches an active OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list or sanctioned jurisdiction:
1. Immediate blocking/freezing of funds prior to settlement.
2. Immediate notification to regulatory authorities within 10 business days.
3. Creation of an immutable audit trail documenting decision rationale.
